## Purpose

stock-flow-backed player economy result application.

## Scope

- In: Delegating troop/resource economy work to `PlayerEconomyAdapter` or
  `evaluatePlayerEconomy(...)`.
- Out: Structure capture, death cleanup, alliance expiry, embargo expiry,
  cluster cleanup.
