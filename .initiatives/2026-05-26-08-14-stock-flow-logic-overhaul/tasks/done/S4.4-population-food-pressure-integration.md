## Purpose

Connect food shortage outputs to population birth/death flows so the minimal
tick loop becomes produce food, feed people, keep leftovers, adjust system,
births, deaths.

## Scope

- In: Population modifiers from `food.shortageRatio`, famine death/birth
  parameters, wartime consumption effects through `FoodSystem`.
- Out: Separate civilian/military stock split.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Full-food population growth matches expected logistic behavior.
- [x] Food shortage reduces births and/or increases deaths according to config.
- [x] Wartime consumption increases food need through declared reads.
- [x] Resulting population deltas remain deterministic.
