## Purpose

Publish addressable war pressure outputs that other systems can read without
direct cross-system mutation.

## Scope

- In: `war.isAtWar`, `war.mobilizedPopulation`,
  `war.foodConsumptionMultiplier`, and optional `war.casualtyFlow` diagnostics.
- Out: Changing attack resolution or combat damage.
