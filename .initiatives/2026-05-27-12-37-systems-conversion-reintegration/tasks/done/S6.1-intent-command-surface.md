## Purpose

preserving `Executor` behavior.

## Scope

- In: Command creation for attacks, retreat, boat, move warship, spawn,
  alliance, emoji, donations, embargo, build, upgrade, delete, pause, quick
  chat, and disconnect.
- Out: AI/nation migration.

- Modify: `src/core/execution/ExecutionManager.ts`, command-related tests.
