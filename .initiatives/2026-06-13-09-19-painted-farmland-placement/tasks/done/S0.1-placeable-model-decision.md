## Working Notes

- Planning baseline created.
- Decision: Farmland is a normal `UnitType` and member of the player-buildable/structure domain.
- Rationale: this reuses existing construction, disabled-unit config, transport, stats, update serialization, and renderer flows keyed from `UnitType`.
- Semantics: 1x1 owned-land placeable, one Farmland per tile, not upgradable, food-oriented production can be minimal/deferred during first placement implementation.
- Details recorded in `CONTRACT.md`.
